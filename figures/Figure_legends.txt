Figure 2
Cancer-specific input scale and evidence landscape. (A) Frozen candidate and label counts. (B) Processed coverage: the fraction of records with at least one configured availability indicator or positive transformed component in the evidence block. (C) Positive-label source composition. Reproducibility support was constructed differently in the two cancer settings: PDAC used GEO cross-cohort support, whereas LUAD used an internal composite of TCGA and DepMap evidence. The LUAD measure does not represent independent external replication. Panel B reports processed coverage of the configured support block, which also includes GEO and Open Targets fields; coverage does not establish independent replication. Actual observation states are examined separately.

Figure 4
PDAC evidence profiles and candidate triage. (A) Support and risk proxies for the five illustrative genes. (B) Dual-axis score density with the same examples. Figure 5
Benchmark enrichment and perturbation diagnostics. (A,B) Uniform-label permutation summaries and observed recovery for LUAD and PDAC, with Holm adjustment across both nulls and all eight tests. Grey points and whiskers show the permutation mean and mean ±1.96 permutation standard deviations; coloured points show observed recovery. (C) Source-role-out priority percentiles where estimable; higher values denote earlier ranks, and both labels and feature permissions change. (D) Benchmark Top-K recovery. (E) Development-positive out-of-fold recovery. (F) Top-50 membership overlap under background resampling and positive-label thinning, with empirical 2.5th–97.5th percentile ranges.
